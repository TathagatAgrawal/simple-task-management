How to use Tasks

Tasks is the firm's shared to-do board. Every piece of work is a card with an owner, a due date and a running log of updates, so everyone can see what's due and where things stand without asking.

Signing in

Make sure Tailscale is connected on your computer, then open the bookmark for Tasks.

A typical day

  1. Check “Due soon”. Start with anything in Overdue, then Due today. When anything is overdue for anyone in the firm, the Overdue card turns red and shows a count, and it names other people's overdue tasks even while you are looking at “My tasks”.
  2. Open a task by clicking its row or card, and read the latest updates.
  3. Start work: drag the card to Doing (or click Doing inside the task) so others know you're on it.
  4. Post updates as things happen: “Emailed client for Form 16”, “Filed, acknowledgement saved”. This is how the team stays informed without asking.
  5. Finish: move it to Done. If it's a recurring task, next period's task appears in To do automatically.
  6. Add new work as it comes in with + New task, assigned to whoever will do it.

Working on a task

Clicking a card (or a row in “Due soon”) opens the task in a panel over the board. Every change saves by itself; there's no Save button. “Saving…” then “Saved ✓” shows at the top of the panel, and if something can't be saved the reason appears right under that field. Setting a due date in the past asks you to confirm first. Deleting a task, or clearing read notifications, also asks first, in a box that says exactly what will go.

Moved a card by mistake? For a few seconds after any move, a bar at the bottom of the page says “Moved … to Done” with an Undo button.

The updates list scrolls on its own, and the box for writing an update always stays at the bottom. The task number (#1 at the top right) is handy for referring to a task in another task's updates.

Moving a task along

There are two ways to change a task's status, and both are recorded in its updates:

  • Drag the card on the board into another column. The column you're over gets a dashed outline; let go to drop it. See the dashed arrow (8) in the board picture.
  • Open the task and click To do, Doing or Done under Status.

Moved something by mistake? Just move it back. Reopening a done task is fine.

Recurring tasks

Monthly GST returns, quarterly TDS, yearly ITRs: set Repeats once and the task keeps coming back.

{{ figure("recurring", 1720, 1800, "A quarterly task just marked Done", pins=[(1, 1230, 266), (2, 24, 771), (3, 24, 826), (4, 1230, 838), (5, 1230, 906)], boxes=[(30, 742, 1162, 59), (30, 797, 1162, 59)], width=780) }} {{ legend([(1, "When a repeating task is marked Done…"), (2, "…the updates record it…"), (3, "…and the next occurrence is created at once, here #13, due a quarter later, with the same title, notes and people. Click the number to open it."), (4, "Repeats shows the schedule. Set it to Never to stop future occurrences."), (5, "Other occurrences lists earlier and later copies of the same task.")]) }}
  • The next due date is counted from the previous due date, not from when you finished. Filing late doesn't shift the schedule.
  • Month ends are handled: a task due on the 31st comes back on the 28th/30th in shorter months, and on the 31st again when the month allows.
  • Some statutory dates aren't evenly spaced. When that happens, just change the due date on the new task.
  • The next task only appears once the current one is done, so if this month's is still open, it stays at the top of Overdue until it's finished.

Finding tasks

The Showing menu at the top left (1 in the board picture) controls both the board and the “Due soon” strip:

  • My tasks: tasks you're assigned to, including ones shared with others.
  • Everyone: the whole firm's work.
  • Unassigned: tasks nobody has picked up yet.
  • A person's name: just their tasks, e.g. to cover for someone on leave.

Your choice is remembered on that computer. If your board looks empty, check this first.

To find a task by name, type in the Search tasks box at the top (press / to jump to it, Esc to clear it). It looks through the tasks in your “Showing” choice, including older finished ones. If you don't see what you expect, try Everyone. The “Due soon” strip says when there is overdue work for other people that “My tasks” is hiding, and one click shows it.

Prefer the keyboard? See Keyboard shortcuts.

Notifications

Whenever someone else changes a task you're on, you get a notification. That covers a new task assigned to you, a change to the title, notes, due date or repeat, a move to another column, a comment, being added or removed, and a deletion. You never get a notification for something you did yourself.

  • A message appears at the top right of the page, under the bell (at the top on a phone), while the board is open. Messages about a new task for you, a changed due date, being taken off a task, or a deleted task stay until you dismiss them; others (a comment, a move, an edit) fade after 15 seconds. Press View to open the task. If several arrive at once you get one message that opens the list. While the bell's list is open you won't get messages, because the changes appear in the list itself.
  • The bell at the top shows how many you haven't read. On a phone it is the Alerts tab. Several changes to one task are grouped into one row. Click it to open the task (everything in the row is marked read). The tick marks it read or unread again, and × removes it. Mark all read works on the whole list, and Clear read asks before it deletes. Notifications you've read are cleared automatically after a month.
  • Choose what you hear about. Open Notification settings from your menu (on a phone, from the Me tab), or press Settings at the top of the bell's list. First, under Alerts on this device, turn alerts on for the phone or computer you're using. Then pick how much you want to hear: Alert and bell (the bell, plus an alert on each device where you've turned alerts on), Bell only (the bell, quietly) or Nothing. That one choice covers everything; to treat some kinds of change differently (assigned to you, a task edited, moved, commented on, you're removed, deleted), open Customise by event. A "Saved ✓" appears beside the choice you changed. Your choices only affect you, and everything starts on Alert and bell.
  • To get an alert on a phone or computer even when the board is closed, press Turn on alerts on this device (in the bell's list, or under Alerts on this device in the settings). Your browser asks once; choose Allow. Do this on each device you want alerts on. On an iPhone or iPad, first add the board to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from that icon. A device with alerts on shows the system's own notification instead of a message on the page; the bell still counts. On a computer, alerts arrive while your browser is running. Send a test checks it works, and Turn off stops it. If you'd rather not have task names show on your lock screen, untick Show the task's name in alerts.
  • You are also told about changes other people make to a task you created, even if you aren't on it.

On your phone

The same app works on a phone, laid out for one hand. Make sure Tailscale is connected, then open the usual address.

Put it on your home screen

  • iPhone: open it in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

It then opens full-screen like any other app. On an iPhone you'll sign in once more the first time you open the home-screen icon.

Finding your way around

  • The tabs at the bottom are Board, Due soon (overdue, due today and the next 3 days; the red number is how many are overdue) and Me (theme, password, people, help and sign out).
  • On the Board, tap To do / Doing / Done to switch columns. The + New task button at the top adds a task, the pill next to the title is the “Showing” filter, and the magnifier opens search. The line just above the columns sums up what is overdue, due today and due in 3 days; tap it to see the list.
  • Tap a card to open it as a full page. Go back with the ‹ button, by swiping right from the left edge, or with your phone's back button or gesture.

Moving tasks without dragging

  • Swipe a card right to move it on: To do → Doing → Done. A task that's already Done goes back to To do.
  • Swipe it left, or press and hold, for a menu: Open, Move to…, Delete.
  • You can also open the task and tap a status button, as on a computer.

Pop-up forms (like a new task) slide up from the bottom; drag them down, or tap outside, to close.

For admins: updates

Admins also see Updates in their menu (it says new version when one is waiting). It shows whether a newer version of the app is available on GitHub and what changed; press Check now to look right away. Looking never changes anything: a new version is installed on the firm's PC with update.ps1.

If you turn on Update automatically at night, the PC does that itself at about 3 am (after the one-time setup update.ps1 -EnableAutoUpdate): it takes a backup, installs the new version, restarts the app, which is unavailable for a minute or two, and checks that all your tasks and people are still there. If the new version won't start, the old one is put back. The same window shows what the last automatic run did. After an update, everyone should refresh the page once (Ctrl+F5).

When the app is updated, everyone who is on an open task gets one unread notification for it in their bell (“assigned you to this task”), so existing work shows up right away. These are bell-only: no message pops up and no desktop alert is sent. Read or clear them like any other.

Problems and questions

“Can't reach the server. Is Tailscale connected?”

Check the Tailscale icon in your taskbar (or menu bar) and make sure it says Connected. If it is and the problem continues, the office PC that runs Tasks may be off or restarting. Ask whoever looks after it.

I can't find a task

Set Showing to Everyone. If it was finished more than 14 days ago, click Show older under Done.

Someone else changed my task

Open it and read the grey lines in Updates. Every change is recorded with who made it and when.

Can I undo a change?

Change it back; there's no separate undo. Deleting a task is the only thing that can't be undone.

A red message appeared at the top

It explains what went wrong, e.g. “A task needs a title.” Nothing was saved; fix it and try again.
